Github HQ

  • Workplace
  • Outdoor
The Github headquarters in the SOMA neighborhood of San Francisco is located in a spacious, three story, 55,000 Sq. Ft. industrial warehouse. Previously a dried fruit packing warehouse in the early 1900’s, Studio Hatch designed the interior to nod to the history of the building yet create a comprehensive work environment for the coding tech company. Concreteworks fabricated square and round tables for the meeting and dining areas and acid-etched chess tables, a large wood and concrete bench, planters, and a custom table with a built-in planter on the roof deck.
