# campus_hedge

User request: create low flowering beds, small bushes/hedges, warm amber path bollards, cafe tables with stools and a community noticeboard, in the established campus language. Authored in metres, ground y=0, +Z front. Original editable Blender source and embedded palette texture.

Breeze is a four-second in-place Blender morph clip. Lower 0.20 m and foundations remain fixed; the application chooses phase and playback, with pause/reduced motion respected.
