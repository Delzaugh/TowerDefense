# Architect Copilot — Keystone, v02

User direction: new Architect Tower with a sense of a Copilot; ignore existing tower references and designs. This revision supersedes the initial monument-like concept.

Architect Copilot — Keystone. A fresh friendly low-poly miniature robot architect who functions as a tower unit. Character first. Oversized softly chamfered warm-ivory cuboid HEAD (roughly 40% total body height), wide deep-teal recessed FACE SCREEN with exactly two luminous cyan rounded rectangular EYES, one slightly raised eye for attentive curiosity; no mouth. Ivory shallow projecting top brow rim integrated into head. Head rear is plain ivory with ONE teal rectangular inset, no face on back. Signature DRAFTING COMPASS CREST mounted on crown: compact inverted-V comprising TWO thick ivory tapered beams with orange ends and ONE orange circular pivot with dark-teal center at apex, forming an open triangular space above head; crest width about 70% of head width, height about 45% of head height. Crown crest is a fitted accessory, not ears, horns or a large body arch. Compact deep-teal torso with ivory side shells, one small centered orange chest trapezoid partly covered by tablet. TWO short articulated teal upper arms and ivory forearms, each ending in one simple ivory mitten hand with a thumb and unified fingers. Both hands visibly hold the sides of ONE wide thick dark-teal BLUEPRINT TABLET in front of belly; tablet tilts up, cyan screen carries one simple white stepped architectural plan glyph. EXACTLY TWO short chunky legs ending in broad ivory wedge BOOTS with teal soles, firmly planted, slight outward stance. No platform/pedestal; freestanding robot companion. Orange ankle accents. Stubby proportions, friendly confident helpful expression, larger head and hands, clear head/body separation. Matte chunky bevels, economical low-poly planes, ivory/deep-teal/cyan/warm-orange palette, studio soft shadows. No buildings on body, no miniature city, no weapons, no cape, no hard hat, no pencil, no extra props, no text on model, no floating parts.
