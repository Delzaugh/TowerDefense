# Field Architect production brief

User selected the compact Keystone Field Architect concept and requests its existing model be rebuilt faithfully, with all visible details, economical triangles and detailed specification comparisons.

Authority: references/keystone_placeable_2026-09-27/keystone_field_architect.png. Coordinated companion references: references/keystone_spec_pack_2026-09-27/. The generated rear views are design inferences, not a second model authority.

Landmarks to preserve: broad deep faceted teal core; low-set dark face and two vertical cyan eyes; ivory brow and side frame; narrowing under-chin keel; a blue upright roll on viewer-left in front view; short orange stylus on viewer-right; three diagram tiles above the brow, database on roll side, service hierarchy on stylus side. The side frame contains two triangular teal-backed recesses. No goggles, arms, fins, desk or loose props.

Construction: changing horizontal body sections rather than an extruded front outline; bent ivory frame following that volume; bowed face; rigid manufactured parts seated on the shell. Large panel areas receive sparse planar topology. Curves use only enough segments to carry the visible outline. Paper spiral, tile symbols, routes, pencil cone/tip, bands and dock are actual geometry; no screen-space stand-ins.

Sequence: inspect primary form with simplified accessory volumes in shared Inspector, compare front/profile/isometric outlines, then add fitted detail. Compare finished runtime renders to spec crops with uniform scaling, documented framing and no axis stretching. Check rear/underside and close joins, then small and phone views. Geometry audit includes per-part triangle allocation and degenerates. No model acceptance is inferred from the concept selection.

Working dimension envelope: width 3.1-3.6m, height 2.5-2.9m, depth 2.3-2.9m, at existing family display scale; retain identity root and existing anchors, resting model bottom at zero. This is an intentional form update, not a promise of a measured 2m runtime placement collider. Simulation still owns placement geometry.

Budget remains 4500 triangles, two meshes/materials, one tiny packed palette. Add royal blue for the accepted rolled-plan pod. The prior r27 source and recipes are preserved in revisions/r27_before_field_architect/.
