# Visual and artifact review

Reviewed all seven full-size sheets after generation, 2026-09-18.

- Each uses the shared dark template, readable title, six labeled views, five palette swatches and three concise notes.
- Unknown Persona geometry/texture values remain hyphens; their UV grids remain empty. Base values were independently read from its registered GLB and matched to the existing validation hash.
- Base rear-three-quarter was corrected to show the vented rear shell with the face hidden.
- Security's Right profile was corrected to face the opposite direction from Left.
- Analyst retains the requirements-card/pencil identity and squat ivory construction. The graph and rejected pointed yellow shell are absent.
- Tester retains the asymmetric large/small diagnostic lenses. Developer retains orange fins; Architect retains the structural arch and square projector.
- Linter's rear modules were corrected to form a pair straddling the rear centerline. Its written brief specifies the exact eight-module spacing to resolve in a future 3D source.
- The campus views remain illustrative scale studies, and every wireframe is labeled illustrative.

These are image-generated concept/production-reference sheets. The Personas are not measured meshes; exact projection, attachment handedness, topology, hidden surfaces, emitter count and material assignments must be resolved in a single authoritative 3D source during production. The extracted Base texture file and measured JSON are the technical evidence, rather than the reconstructed image of its atlas on the sheet.

The companion briefs cover accepted gameplay separately from proposed animation and construction. The task did not edit Blender sources, runtime models or gameplay.
