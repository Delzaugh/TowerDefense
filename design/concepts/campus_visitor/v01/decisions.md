# Campus visitor character concepts — v01

## User direction

- Design an ordinary human character for Tower.
- The character is a campus background visitor, not a Human Tower or supporting character.
- Revise the first concept to have a lighter skin tone and create a female version.

## Current concept sheets

- `campus_visitor_male_light_v01.png` — lighter warm beige skin, short dark hair.
- `campus_visitor_female_light_v01.png` — same skin tone and outfit, chin-length dark bob.

Both sheets use the same moss-green open overshirt, cream T-shirt, straight navy trousers and muted terracotta sneakers. They show front, left side, rear and three-quarter views. Their everyday clothing and low-detail silhouette keep the visitors visually quieter than gameplay Towers and Problems.

## Provenance and scope

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-24. The existing Bert Breugelmans Inspector render was used as a style reference for the first concept only, not as an identity or geometry source. The male light-skin sheet edits the first concept; the female sheet uses that revision as its design reference. The image prompts are retained in the task history.

These are concept images for review, not measured orthographic drawings or finished Blender assets. Gender presentation, proportions, hair volume, color swatches and garment seams are proposed art choices. No asset ID, category registration, triangle budget, rig, animation clips or gameplay behavior has been approved by these sheets.
