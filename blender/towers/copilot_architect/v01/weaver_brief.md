# Weaver — application architect copilot

## User direction
The user rejected the sphere-like r23 and asks for a more creative tower-defense
copilot. Earlier feedback rejected vertical slab bodies, roof/top fins and weak
application-architect identity. These explicit corrections govern this redesign;
the original Pavilion sheets and generated exploration are design inputs only.

## Skeptical concept selection
The generated board explores Keystone, Fold and Weaver. Keystone still depends on
a side tablet. Fold replaces the ball with two broad badge planes. Weaver makes
the role a visible action: bringing application, service and data together.
Develop Weaver, reducing its symmetrical robot-arm appearance through staggered
brackets and different node shapes. Keep the bright friendly face and five-role
palette. Avoid a roof, sphere, weapon or decorative floating screens.

## Form landmarks before details
- Upper face: wide truncated triangle, tilted across the body and pitched upward;
  genuinely deep tapering head shell behind it, not a flat face-shaped slab.
- Lower core: folded keel with changing octagonal sections and a receding tip;
  no constant-width vertical run. Rear extends behind the shoulder sockets.
- Two thick open brackets: staggered in height and depth, attached at the back
  and returning toward the lower core. Large negative spaces survive game view.
- Three functional foreground volumes: central orange application, lower cream
  service at one side and taller cream database at the other, joined by cyan
  routing seated on dark structural rails. No roof-mounted diagram.
- Readable hierarchy: face first, orange app second, bracket silhouette third.
  Symbols clarify close viewing; the role must not depend on them alone.

## Construction and review
Use varying sections and locally fitted hard-surface parts. Review the blockout
front/side/isometric and black silhouette before software glyphs. Review the final
export in close/reverse/underside views and at phone/game scale; record repairs
and hash-bound findings. Model acceptance stays pending, animation stays deferred.
