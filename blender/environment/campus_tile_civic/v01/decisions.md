# Civic Plaza brief and decisions

## User direction

Create a new civic campus section as an independently placeable tile and decor assembly. Preserve all existing roads and the central lab. This source is owned independently; shared recipes and other assets remain untouched.

## Primary form and landmarks

Flat-top regular hexagon radius 18 m; footprint 36 by 31.1769 m. Identical existing shell, trim, y=1.20 m surface, surface anchor and six edge anchors. All top remains perfectly level. Broad staggered 3 m ashlar joints define a restrained civic square.

## Construction and materials

Shared base geometry reused without changing its helper. Paving lives in an opaque packed 512 px atlas, with no overlay geometry or baked roads. Outer 1.4 m is constant #354B64; the paving fades smoothly beyond that into large low-contrast stone slabs. Structural frame swatches and roughness .92 match the foundation.
