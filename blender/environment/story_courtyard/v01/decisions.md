## Primary form and staging landmarks

Original authored design follows the current Visual Asset Guide. No third-party
models or image-generation references were used. The 24 m sandstone courtyard
provides a warm backdrop with a coffee-house facade near z=-7, awning, inset cafe
windows, quiet side buildings and deliberately peripheral vegetation/furniture.
Ground and facade proportions were blocked out before fittings: ground x±12,
z=-10..8; back cafe height5.8; taller framing wings atx±9.5. The central acting
zone x=-2..4,z=0 and escape corridor along +x to12 remain free of furniture.
The model has a local paving surface y=0; its substrate extends below the origin.
This is recorded as a deliberate non-grounded modular origin in the manifest.

## Construction and materials

Soft single-segment beveled architecture contrasts with deliberately broad
rectangular windows and angular foliage. Window surrounds are fitted layers,
with glass panels forward of their seats; awning stripes are contiguous closed
sloping prisms with a short separate valance. Chair seats, pedestals, shutters,
planter lips and roof coping have purpose rather than generic box decoration.
The palette reuses campus chalk/slate/teal and family graphite, supplemented by
warm sandstone, terracotta and coffee wood. One packed64×4 opaque palette atlas
and one material serve six batched static meshes. No clips or skeleton are needed.
