# Thinking Octocat bronze interpretation

User direction, 2026-09-30: create reusable GitHub office/campus models and a disposable assembled prototype.

## Reference and primary form

Original low-poly interpretation of Tony Jaramillo Thinktocat: big round head, two cat ears, lowered thoughtful face, chin supported by curled tentacle, crossed elbow tentacle, two seated feet, swept rear tail, white tapered plinth. Bronze palette keeps sculptural identity. No existing character asset was edited.

Research references: thinktocat-03.jpg, thinktocat-01.png. Exact as-built office measurements are unavailable; this is an original friendly low-poly interpretation. The requested target size is approximately 2.5 × 3.3 × 2.5 metres in X/Y/Z; furniture ensembles may have slight practical variation. Resolve broad body silhouette, repeated rhythm and real negative spaces before fittings.

## Interfaces and delivery

Center X/Z; floor contact Y=0; front+Z. Static environment, no rig or invented clips. All component transforms joined/applied beneath root. One 64×4 packed palette texture with deliberate padded swatches, no photo textures. Opaque plate treatment is an intentional readability/performance interpretation for suspended contribution art. Author review and technical validation are separate; explicit user acceptance remains pending.

## Detail refinement, 2026-09-30

Explicit user direction: "The thinking Octocat could use a good polishing on details." Canonical editable source hash matched recorded procedural authority before rebuilding. Revision 6 retains plinth footprint, +Z facing, static root and bronze/chalk palette; previous sources/exports are retained by guarded delivery milestones.
