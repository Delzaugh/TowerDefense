## User direction and scope

User authorized a reusable 3D GitHub office/campus kit plus disposable prototype on 2026-09-30. This source is an original friendly low-poly interpretation, not a measured as-built reconstruction. ## Reference priorities and primary form

5 m wide interpreted front portal: pale projecting canopy, slender steel diagonal supports, paired transparent doors and charcoal fitting frame. Small green canopy edge introduces the current brand family. Portal has no opaque wall blocking its doorway; model does not invent an official logo.

Public references: [original architect](https://fm-arch.com/workitems/github/), [historic entrance photograph and documentation](../../../../docs/research/github-offices-2026-09-30/report.html), [contemporary atrium](https://interiorarchitects.com/projects/githubatrium/) and [roof concrete furniture context](https://concreteworks.com/projects/github-hq/). The dated research report, source registry and photographs remain the provenance record. Examined contact-exterior, contact-original-architect and contact-atrium sheets before construction. The historical 15.24 m height is a verified cue; footprint, floor intervals, openings and campus connections are authored interfaces.

## Construction and materials

Primary outline resolved from the documented warehouse/atrium/container vocabulary before trim. Arches use extruded profile surfaces, real voids and full-depth intrados; borders are radial continuous profiles, not crossed beveled bars. Repeated windows share centers, springlines and sill heights. Boards, posts, frame rails and glazing have distinct seats and thickness. A 64 × 4 packed sRGB palette supplies the opaque structure; role UVs sample padded swatch centers. Glass is a practical alpha exception needed for furnished interior views; no refraction, no fake opaque backing. Static environment architecture requires no skeleton or invented clips.
