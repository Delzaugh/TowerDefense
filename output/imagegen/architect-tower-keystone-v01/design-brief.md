## Shared design

Architect Tower — Keystone. Entirely new non-humanoid low-poly tower. A squat chamfered square dark-teal plinth with ivory beveled upper rim supports a broad teal drafting dais. Above the dais rises one huge inverted-V drafting compass arch: TWO thick ivory tapered flat beams, joined at top by ONE orange circular pivot with dark teal center. The arch has a large open triangular negative space. Each beam terminates at opposite left/right rear corners of the dais; one slim warm orange drafting nib at the end of the model's right beam. Between the beams, on the dais, exactly THREE simple ivory rectangular architectural massing blocks, staggered short/tall/medium, arranged as a compact stepped cluster. One broad cyan inset on the front face of the tallest block, not a face or eyes. At the FRONT edge of the dais, one thick tilted dark teal blueprint tablet with a cyan surface showing a single simple white stepped plan outline; no text. Rear of tablet is opaque teal. Base has one centered orange trapezoid on its front, plain rear. Compass and model cluster are integral tower structure; blueprint tablet is the sole distinct fitted accessory. No humanoid, no limbs, no robot face, no weapons, no hats, no floating holograms, no ornament, no bolts, no tiny decals. Chunky economical beveled geometry, matte flat colors, friendly software architecture metaphor. Entire height around twice plinth width visually, no measured dimensions. Five palette roles ivory, deep teal, slate navy, cyan, warm orange. Consistent silhouette and part counts, near-orthographic views.
