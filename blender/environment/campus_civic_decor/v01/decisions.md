# Civic Plaza brief and decisions

## User direction

Create a new civic campus section as an independently placeable tile and decor assembly. Preserve all existing roads and the central lab. This source is owned independently; shared recipes and other assets remain untouched.

## Primary form and landmarks

The decor origin matches the tile centre. A pale continuous portal monument stands at local (-4,11.95), framed by two inward-facing timber benches. Low planting occupies the rear corners. Everything lies within local x[-6,-2], z[11,14]. The open portal has a consistent 0.24 m side width and an explicitly modeled opening; a suspended aqua crystal supplies one restrained focal feature.

## Construction and materials

One mesh and one opaque material with a packed 64 by 8 px semantic palette. Plinth rests at local y0 for runtime placement at y1.2. Benches have separate support members, timber seat slats and rear uprights; soil is inset below visible planter rims. The portal is a single continuous ring mesh with true thickness. No animation, gameplay geometry or labels.
