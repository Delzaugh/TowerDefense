# Sit-in bookshelf and reading carrels

User direction, 2026-09-30: create reusable GitHub office/campus models and a disposable assembled prototype.

## Reference and primary form

Pale wood sit-in shelving: central true arch negative space, side reading bays and upper open shelves. Burgundy folded carrel screens in front capture the expansion identity.

Research references: rapt-expansion-02.jpg, rapt-expansion-01.jpg. Exact as-built office measurements are unavailable; this is an original friendly low-poly interpretation. The requested target size is approximately 6 × 3.2 × 2.2 metres in X/Y/Z; furniture ensembles may have slight practical variation. Resolve broad body silhouette, repeated rhythm and real negative spaces before fittings.

## Interfaces and delivery

Center X/Z; floor contact Y=0; front+Z. Static environment, no rig or invented clips. All component transforms joined/applied beneath root. One 64×4 packed palette texture with deliberate padded swatches, no photo textures. Opaque plate treatment is an intentional readability/performance interpretation for suspended contribution art. Author review and technical validation are separate; explicit user acceptance remains pending.
