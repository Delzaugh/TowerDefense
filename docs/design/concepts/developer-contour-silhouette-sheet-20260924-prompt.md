DESIGN: pristine warm-white background, charcoal-black drawings, minimal professional industrial-design turnaround sheet. Small top title "DEVELOPER — SHAPE STUDY". Two aligned rows of SIX views each, with ample white space and consistent object scale. Upper row heading small "SILHOUETTES"; lower row heading small "CONTOURS". A thin light-gray horizontal divider separates rows. Angle labels below each drawing, identically ordered in both rows: FRONT, FRONT-LEFT, LEFT, BACK, BACK-RIGHT, RIGHT. Fit all drawings comfortably without clipping, sharp contours, no decorative graphics or other text. The silhouette and corresponding contour directly beneath it must have EXACTLY matching outer shape and angle.

UPPER ROW: six solid charcoal-black silhouettes. Completely flat filled shapes; NO internal lines, highlights, shading, eyes, badges, lens colors, gray panels or texture. Preserve actual background-visible negative spaces between rear-swept fin tips where open to the outside; do not turn surface recesses into holes. All opaque assembled parts merge into one solid silhouette. This is a strict shape readability test. Front and rear silhouettes should appropriately resemble each other for the same object viewed in opposite exact projections.

LOWER ROW: the same six views drawn as precise black contour line art with white interiors. Strong outer contour and slightly thinner lines indicating ONLY major anatomical boundaries: orange shell outline, three rear-swept fins and open channels, thick goggle rims and their bridge, face display and angular chin rim, octagonal temple housing and inset badge plate, two rear ventilation slots where visible. Simplify bevel detail. No triangulation, wireframe, dense facet lines, hatching, shadows, fills or gray gradients. Two eye bars and brace emblem may be minimal outline shapes where visible, subordinate to the structural lines. Show clean joined contours, never tangled sketch strokes. Outer contour about twice the weight of inner construction lines. The point is easy modeling reference for broad masses and major seams.

Temple housings are octagons, not circular headphones. Keep the fin silhouette long and swept back, in reference proportions. These are the exact same coherent object rotated, not alternate designs.
