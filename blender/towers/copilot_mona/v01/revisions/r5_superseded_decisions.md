## Reference priorities and primary form
Official GitHub Brand Toolkit contemporary Mona sheet governs the head: rounded square cheeks, short cat ears at the top corners, shallow central forehead dip, pink face with cheek lobes, cream eye whites, large plum pupils, tiny nose/smile and two whiskers per cheek. It specifies purple/pink visually, not numeric hex values. Texture colours are chosen from that reference's midtones; coloured lighting is not copied as geometry.
Official Octocat 2.0 illustration supplies the full-body interpretation: narrow torso beneath a much broader head, two tentacle arms, two grounded supporting legs and one tail. The current brand sheet supplies head-only views, so the combined full body is an authored adaptation, not an official GitHub full-body source. No human fingers, extra limbs, clothes or puddle geometry.
Authored height 1.8m, positive Z facing in glTF. Blender head center 2.34 before uniform scale, half width .95, half height .79, depth .72; head side volume remains rounded. Ear tips at approximately x +/-.855, z 3.27. Eye centers x +/-.375, z 2.305. Nose z 2.025. Body spans roughly z .0–1.65. Preserve negative spaces around curled arms and split legs, with a seated neck inside the head base. Primary-form blockout is inspected in front, side and isometric runtime views before final detail delivery.

## Construction and materials
Adapted and localized the project's existing procedural Octocat construction helpers into this asset's independent build.py. Head and ears form one remeshed surface; torso and exactly five tentacles form one separate continuous surface. Facial details conform to the actual head BVH; cups seat against the reduced body BVH. Named anatomical construction curves remain outside the root for future editing. Two opaque materials: packed 512x512 painted head gradient and packed 128x64 gradient/semantic atlas. Solid swatches are declared in asset.json; gradient regions are intentionally not called solid swatches. Palette roles retain cream eyes, plum eyes/nose/smile, pink ears and pink cups. Purple/magenta gradient continuity is intentional. No gameplay code or presentation tint.
