TESTER — "Quality Lens"
A porcelain-white and forest-green softly faceted hexagonal head. Two thick equal-sized HEXAGONAL diagnostic goggles with green frames over the visible face eyes. Broad green U-shaped calibration brackets wrap around both temple modules; one bold white tick on the green left module. Geometric, precise, upright and observant. Large clean lens geometry, no round monocle and no extra chart.

ANALYST — "Brief Builder"
A low horizontal SLATE-BLUE and warm-ivory head designed like a compact document binder, with gently layered rounded rectangular shell plates and a broad flat crown. Elegant connected ivory rectangular reading spectacles along the brow, two cyan eyes below. One temple incorporates THREE thick oversized cream work-ticket tabs neatly staggered, with only a few broad teal horizontal strokes; a short copper refinement pencil clips across their lower edge. Tickets are safely seated and form a restrained stepped silhouette, not flying paper. A chunky friendly requirements organizer. No graph, no yellow, no pointed dome, no check marks.

SECURITY — "Sentinel"
A broad friendly shield-shaped head: chamfered pentagonal upper silhouette with a blunt broad lower edge, royal indigo/navy inner shell and a thick warm-white protective perimeter. The shell itself conveys a shield, no separate handheld prop. White rectangular protective goggles tucked into the upper brow, compact dark face with cyan eyes. Two chunky white temple blocks with integrated circular cyan scan lenses, making a different silhouette from the reference's jaw-heavy design. A small blue shield emblem on one cheek. ARCHITECT — "Stack Compiler"

A square cyan structural-packet aperture is recessed into the highest rear terrace, visibly pointing diagonally upward. The silhouette is a compact stepped ziggurat/building with strongly staggered side buttresses, not the original arch and not a cannon. A composed sturdy hovering structure with a clear friendly face.

LINTER AGENT — "Rule Crown"
A squat forest-mint and charcoal rounded head surrounded by EIGHT broad petal-like radial emitter modules arranged horizontally as a scalloped eight-point mechanical crown around the upper perimeter. Each petal ends in one dark square outlet. The upper face has two small angular mint goggles and visible cyan eyes underneath. Visible front and side modules plus sensible rear ones, eight total intended.
