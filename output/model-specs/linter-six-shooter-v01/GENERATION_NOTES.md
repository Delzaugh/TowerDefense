Preserve its squat low-poly floating puck tower: broad lime-yellow octagonal shell, dark charcoal stepped lower body and inset top, integrated dark visor with two narrow slanted cyan eyes, two tiny cyan vertical face marks, and boxy lime-framed dark recessed shooter pods. Refine the original eight-pod design to EXACTLY SIX IDENTICAL SHOOTER PODS mounted radially in one horizontal ring, with their outward centerline azimuths spaced by EXACTLY 60 DEGREES. The octagonal body may remain octagonal; use a distinct radial mounting ring to reconcile six evenly spaced pod directions with the shell facets. True straight front/back/profile projections and useful three-quarter views; same object, scale, elevation, lighting and material in all cells. Dark navy background, restrained studio lighting, large crisp models occupying ~80% of each cell, small clear labels only. Keep existing silhouette and colors, no legs, no extra attachments, no projectile effects, no scene, no statistics, no scenery, no title. Back-side construction is conceptual. Crucial geometry: exactly six pods around the ring, no extra cylinders, openings, or doubled pods. No text other than the six view labels.
