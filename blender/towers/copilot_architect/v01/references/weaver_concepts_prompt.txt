Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE wide concept exploration board, three distinctly different designs for an APPLICATION ARCHITECT COPILOT character in a friendly software-themed tower-defense game. Three large full-body three-quarter views on neutral pale blue-gray, evenly spaced, labels A KEYSTONE / B FOLD / C WEAVER. These must be memorable asymmetric low-poly GAME CHARACTERS, dimensional assembled volumes with open gaps, confident design, not generic robots. Friendly dark face with two simple cyan eyes, cream structural casing, deep teal body, small orange accents. Matte flat-shaded bevels, clean broad shapes, executable as 2500-4500 triangle models. A KEYSTONE: floating angular keystone face pod, held in an offset thick C-shaped drafting bracket, lower diagonal folded body, one chunky architectural plan cartridge projects from a flank. Bracket should integrate around cheek and under body, not be an ornamental roof. B FOLD: a compact living folded blueprint, wide friendly wedge face in cream faceted envelope, asymmetrically folded teal/cream body like interlocking origami volumes, a large inset three-node software diagram on its slanted chest; tapered offset lower wedge and small orange connector; no huge vertical flat planes. C WEAVER: a clever faceted triangular central face with two staggered thick architectural bracket arms around its lower sides, supporting a small orange application block connected to two cream service blocks, silhouette has pronounced negative spaces, active purposeful leaning pose. All three are copilot helpers, not humanoid soldiers, towers or buildings. No spheres or eggs, no box torso, no fins, no roof slabs, no hats, no antennae, no weapons, no giant glowing holograms, no thin floating decorative panels, no pencil-as-gun, no feet, no greeble noise. Realistic structural thickness and deep front-to-back volume. Strong medium-size-readable silhouettes. This is new concept exploration, not replication of prior designs.
