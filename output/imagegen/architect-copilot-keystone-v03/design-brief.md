# Architect Copilot — Keystone v03

## User direction

On 2026-09-27 the user asked to look at the Copilot Base for inspiration. This explicitly updates the earlier instruction to ignore existing designs for this named reference. The new direction supersedes v02's full-body robot anatomy.

## Inspiration actually inspected

The copilot_base v02 manifest, decisions, and validation renders: iso.png, front.png, side.png and rear.png. No previous Architect concept in the project's persona studies was inspected or used.

Observed Base features: broad rounded helmet-like floating head; two thick separate forehead goggles; two chunky side pods; dark lower face with two vertical cyan eyes; forward chin rim. Base is limb-free. Its recorded persona-exploration decisions allow different colors and redesigned goggles.

## Proposed design

Architect Copilot — Keystone, Base-inspired revision. Preserve the supplied actual Copilot Base's broad rounded helmet/head-only anatomy, faceted oval dome, forward lower chin rim, pair of thick separate squared goggles resting ABOVE its face, two chunky side pods, large dark lower face plate with exactly two vertical cyan capsule eyes and no mouth. The whole character is this one compact floating head: NO torso, arms, hands, legs, boots or pedestal. New architect identity: warm ivory main shell with a deep-teal lower rim and deep-teal side pods; oversized forehead goggles have thick ivory rims, a dark-teal bridge and solid muted cyan lens surfaces; keep goggles clearly distinct from the two bright cyan eyes on the dark navy face beneath. ONE small architect compass crest perched centrally on crown behind goggles, height about 20% of main head height: two stout ivory beams with orange tips joined at one orange circular top pivot, open triangular center; never horns or antenna pair. On character LEFT side pod only, ONE inset dark-teal blueprint badge with a simple white three-step plan outline, flush in pod surface (no extra tablet, no writing); opposite pod has one plain orange disk. The head rear is clean ivory with exactly three broad dark-teal horizontal vent bars low on its center. Friendly attentive expression and very subtle inquisitive tilt only in hero, neutral orientation in turnarounds. Restrained ivory, deep teal, navy, cyan and warm-orange color scheme. Soft matte economical low-poly forms. Goggles and compass crest are fitted assemblies; blueprint badge is surface graphic. Preserve base family proportions and strong goggle/cheek silhouette. No building blocks, freestanding tools, hardhat, robot body or handheld equipment.
