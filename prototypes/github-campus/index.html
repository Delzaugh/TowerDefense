ABOUT THE STUDY

GitHub, translated into space.

This disposable campus brings together the historic Brannan warehouse, orange container studios, a quiet oak library, rooftop chess and a contemporary green atrium.

It is a designed interpretation of documented GitHub spaces and brand cues. The campus layout, dimensions beyond documented building height, and furniture arrangements are proposed for this model kit.

The models remain separate, reusable assets. The bookshelf and work lounge details follow the supplied office photographs. Three mural panels interpret the playful character and universe themes in Tony Jaramillo’s art direction.

Research: public architect, maker, contractor and GitHub brand references, collected September 2026. No third-party photographs are used as scene textures.
