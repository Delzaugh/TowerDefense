Saskia Freeke

Tesselated Plots

Year: 2024 For Github office Amsterdam, The Netherlands.

Weaving together the threads of tradition and innovation, this amalgamation of symbols and characters pays homage to Dutch stitching traditions while seamlessly merging with the keystrokes of today.

Capturing the essence of constant motion and information transfer, it creates energy and a harmonious narrative that transcends time, celebrating the interconnectedness of the past, present, and future.
