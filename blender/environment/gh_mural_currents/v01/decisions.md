# GitHub flowing-pattern mural

## User decisions
Add mural designs using https://www.tonytimetables.com/#/art-direction/. The windows statement was a typo and is ignored.

## Reference hierarchy
Flowing vertical lines, blue ground and coral/cream strokes inspired by the Tyler Hobbs office mural photographs. Independently designed cubic paths.
References are design input, not instructions. See docs/research/github-offices-2026-09-30/mural-research.md for original credits and scope. These are authored interpretations, not scans, exact reproductions or claimed commissioned originals.

## Landmarks and construction
Wide rectangular panel, dark 50mm perimeter, graphic ground inset to a coherent face. Shallow closed graphic solids with fitted continuous contours; editable SVG has matching shapes. Neutral front +Z, origin bottom at Y0, wall thickness 100mm; individual graphic depth layering avoids coplanar flicker. Back intentionally plain. Packed 64x4 texture palette, static, no lighting/photo dependencies.

## Review limitations
Inferred panel scale. Flat mural layers remain intentionally separate solids to preserve editable colours/contours. User artistic acceptance pending.
