## Limits

Back surfaces, component depths and concealed contacts are inferred. Image generation can produce minor contour and perspective differences between sheets. Original selected front image governs when resolving disagreements. This pack contains no production mesh, actual UVs, measured dimensions or animation data.

No Blender process was used in this reference-pack task.
