# Hexagonal campus foundation
User decision, 2026-09-17: campus must be at least twice as large, extensible through joinable base chunks; explore hexagon motifs; lock the camera and zoom out at least 50% more.
Design choice: large flat-topped hexagonal foundations; retain readable straight and curved routes with restrained hexagonal wayfinding marks. A seven-tile starting cluster provides room for additional buildings.
Each tile: regular hexagon of circumradius 12 m, 24 m vertex-to-vertex, 20.78460969 m between opposite flat edges, surface y=1.20 m. All six top edges are flush; the perimeter inlay changes color without a raised curb, so adjoining tiles have no lip or double rim. Root bottom y=0. Six named edge midpoint anchors define neighbor attachment.
Use axial centers x=18q, z=sqrt(3)*12*(r+q/2). Neighboring tiles share an entire 12 m edge at identical elevation. Seven tiles span 60 × 62.354 m and cover 2618.86 m², over three times the original clipped platform area.
The one-piece campus_platform v01 remains preserved as the earlier reference. This independently registered tile is the new primary surface.

## Textured surface and future terrain family — revision 8

User request, 2026-09-18: give plain base tiles texture while allowing future park and small hill tiles.
The delivered top is matte mineral composite in the approved slate palette: restrained mottling and fine aggregate, fading into a quiet 1.4 m perimeter band. No grid is drawn on the tile surface. Rotations in 60-degree increments vary texture repetition without changing the regular hexagon or its joins. The first texture pass (revision 7) was reduced because broad contrast looked too watery at campus scale.

Editable texture lives in the Blender source as a packed 512 × 512 atlas generated by surface.py. The atlas combines a spatial top region and four solid frame swatches. Automatic solid-palette repacking is disabled to preserve spatial UVs; normal guarded source-hash/export validation remains active. Solid swatches remain editable in Inspector, while the spatial top must be changed through the packed image or its source recipe.

Geometry remains 108 triangles, one mesh, one material, one texture; radius 18 m and height 1.2 m are unchanged. Future park/hill variants should share this structural frame, six edge anchors and level boundary; interior grass, stone or raised terrain can vary. Park and hill are design directions only, not delivered models. Details: docs/design/Campus_Tile_System.md.
