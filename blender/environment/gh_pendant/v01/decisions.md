# Suspended industrial light rig

User direction, 2026-09-30: create reusable GitHub office/campus models and a disposable assembled prototype.

## Reference and primary form

Three hanging warehouse lamps, distinct black shades and visible warm bulbs/cables. Origin at lowest bulb; raise asset to selected ceiling height.

Research references: original-architect-03.jpg, original-architect-15.jpg. Exact as-built office measurements are unavailable; this is an original friendly low-poly interpretation. The requested target size is approximately 3 × 1.6 × 1.4 metres in X/Y/Z; furniture ensembles may have slight practical variation. Resolve broad body silhouette, repeated rhythm and real negative spaces before fittings.

## Interfaces and delivery

Center X/Z; floor contact Y=0; front+Z. Static environment, no rig or invented clips. All component transforms joined/applied beneath root. One 64×4 packed palette texture with deliberate padded swatches, no photo textures. Opaque plate treatment is an intentional readability/performance interpretation for suspended contribution art. Author review and technical validation are separate; explicit user acceptance remains pending.

Author repair: phone oblique view hid bulbs below black shades. A warm metal rim and separate restrained emission material make the luminaire readable under neutral and dark viewer backgrounds. Both materials reuse the same packed 64x4 palette image; emission is visual lamp illumination, no gameplay state.
