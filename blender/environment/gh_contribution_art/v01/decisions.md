# Green contribution-plate atrium artwork

User direction, 2026-09-30: create reusable GitHub office/campus models and a disposable assembled prototype.

## Reference and primary form

Static suspended green contribution plates in a 23 by 17 lattice with uneven depth and cat negative-space motif. The source installation resolves Mona from a particular angle; this modular interpretation resolves a cat motif from front and becomes abstract from oblique views. Opaque plates intentionally simplify polycarbonate at phone scale; no unsupported moving mechanism.

Research references: atrium-01.jpg, atrium-03.jpg. Exact as-built office measurements are unavailable; this is an original friendly low-poly interpretation. The requested target size is approximately 8 × 6 × 1.5 metres in X/Y/Z; furniture ensembles may have slight practical variation. Resolve broad body silhouette, repeated rhythm and real negative spaces before fittings.

## Interfaces and delivery

Center X/Z; floor contact Y=0; front+Z. Static environment, no rig or invented clips. All component transforms joined/applied beneath root. One 64×4 packed palette texture with deliberate padded swatches, no photo textures. Opaque plate treatment is an intentional readability/performance interpretation for suspended contribution art. Author review and technical validation are separate; explicit user acceptance remains pending.
